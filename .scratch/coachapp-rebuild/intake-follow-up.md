# Follow up on client intake

When an accepted client has not completed their intake, the coach needs to remind them or adjust what is expected without losing submitted answers. This is a brief phone or desktop task entered from Client overview and the Onboarding workbench link.

Compared another management page with a compact pending-intake card: keep Send reminder beside the status, email recipient and a quieter Manage intake action. A dialog edits due date and normal/high priority, with separate explicit dismiss/reopen confirmations. Explain priority as the client's intake priority rather than promising unsupported Workbench ordering. Dismissal removes the intake from outstanding work and prevents submission until reopened; it does not invent answers or complete onboarding. Inactive and unaccepted clients keep the appropriate access/invitation route. Submitted answers retain the existing review screen.

Use generated assignment types and partial PATCHes. Pass the observed assignment status so a concurrent submission cannot be overwritten; serialize coach updates with submission completion. Preserve date/priority drafts after failures. Recover uncertain state updates by reading the client's intake; never automatically retry reminder email after a lost response because the existing endpoint has no delivery receipt. Show an explicit uncertain outcome and require the coach to choose whether to send again. A successful reminder means delivery was requested, not proof the client received it. No messages are sent by the agent to real clients; browser tests use disposable accounts and local mail.

Invalidate intake, client lists/details and Workbench counts/queues after status changes and review. Keep pending mutations within the current account. Check accepted/pending/inactive, no-email, dismissed/reopened, submitted, stale updates, loss/retry, keyboard/date fields and both 375px and 1280px. Retained query failures keep useful data visible and prevent actions until refreshed. No commits.
