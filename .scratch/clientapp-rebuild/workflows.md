# Supported client workflows

* Accept a direct coaching invitation and sign in with email OTP.
* Open Today and follow current training and nutrition guidance.
* Start, resume, complete and correct planned or freestyle workouts.
* Record, share and correct meals with durable history.
* Exchange text and supported media with the coach, including source-linked notes.
* Maintain personal details.

Native credentials and draft recovery remain part of these flows. Device push delivery remains outside the supported API.
